Comprehensive Defect Detection

Our system identifies and classifies multiple types of hull defects using both supervised and unsupervised detection methods.

Severe Corrosion

Deep material loss and structural weakening requiring immediate intervention or steel renewal.

Moderate Corrosion

Visible surface degradation with measurable material loss. Warrants monitoring and planned maintenance.

Mild Corrosion

Early-stage rust and surface oxidation. Typically treatable with spot-blasting and coating touch-up.

Coating Failure

Peeling, cracking, blistering, or chalking of anti-corrosive and anti-fouling coating systems.

Marine Fouling

Barnacles, algae, and biological growth increasing drag and fuel consumption. Assessed per IMO guidelines.

Good Condition

Hull surface is intact with no significant defects. Coating system is functional and within service limits.

Dual Detection Pipeline

Choose from two complementary detection approaches based on your needs.

YOLO Segmentation

Supervised • Deep Learning

Instance segmentation model trained on hull corrosion datasets. Outputs pixel-level masks and bounding boxes with per-defect confidence scores. Best for precise localization of known defect types.

Eigen Tree

Unsupervised • Classical

Unsupervised pruned decision-tree hierarchy using eigen decomposition (Chliveros et al., 2023). Requires no training data, making it ideal for novel hull conditions and out-of-distribution scenarios.
